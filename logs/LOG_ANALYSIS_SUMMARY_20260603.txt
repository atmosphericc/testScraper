PURCHASE FAILURE ROOT-CAUSE
================================================================================
The ATC 401 _ERR_AUTH_DENIED (T83072242) signature, every occurrence:
    5/29 02:04  95298172  401  (headers 2h stale)
    5/29 02:15  95298172  401  (headers FRESH 8-15s -> not a staleness problem)
    5/29 02:25  95267143  201  <-- cart succeeded; failed later at checkout (429)
    6/2  02:28  95267143  401  <-- ETB now also 401s
T83072242 / _ERR_AUTH_DENIED is Target's IBM-Commerce auth-denied path: the
auth context attached to the cart_items POST was rejected. Cookies being present
and unexpired (watchdog "healthy") does NOT mean the auth/Shape context is
accepted for a state-changing cart write. Leading hypotheses (from 5/29, still
open): (i) ATC POST missing a PDP-derived token because TARGET_FORCE_QTY_1=true
skips the PDP nav; (ii) the warmup-tab visitor session has drifted from the
logged-in member session; (iii) account-level flag/limit. The 6/2 spread to the
ETB makes (ii)/(iii) more likely than a per-SKU rule.

   - Concretely: set TARGET_SWEEPS_PER_SEC=0.5 in run_bot_with_nightly_restart
     .bat (overrides the app.py:46 default of 3.0).
   - Add a CLOAK CANARY: during any known in-stock window, read the same TCIN
     through (a) the BD pool and (b) a clean residential IP; pool=OOS while
     clean=IN_STOCK proves cloaking. Run continuously as an alarm. This is the
     only way to DEFINITIVELY settle cloak-vs-cache-miss.
     >>> IMPLEMENTED 2026-06-03 (read-only): src/monitoring/stock_check_resilient
     .py now runs two diagnostic loops every 30s, gated by STOCK_DIAG_PROBES
     (default on): _canary_loop (host-direct clean-identity read -> logs

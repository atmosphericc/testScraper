PURCHASE FAILURE ROOT-CAUSE
================================================================================

FAILURE A: 95298172 booster bundle, ATC HTTP 401 _ERR_AUTH_DENIED (T83072242)
  Both drops failed identically. Drop 1 had headers age 7191s (over 2 hours
  stale - warmup ring evicted everything during the long quiet stretch from
  midnight start to 2 AM drop). Drop 2 had fresh headers (age 8-15s) and STILL
  got 401. So this is NOT primarily a stale-headers problem.

  errorCode T83072242 is Target's IBM Commerce auth-denied path - meaning the
  account auth context attached to the POST was rejected. Combined with the
  observation that AUTH_FIX only found device cookies (_pxhd, GuestLocation,
  kampyle*) and "No cookies widened", the most plausible cause is one of:

    (i)  The ATC POST is missing/sending a stale x-api-key, x-request-tracing
         header, or the X-Reference-Header derived from a PDP nav that never
         happened (TARGET_FORCE_QTY_1=true skips PDP nav -> no PDP-derived
         tokens).
    (ii) The visitor session bound to the warmup tab has drifted from the
         logged-in account session, so the F5 Shape signature attaches to a
         GUEST visitor while the body claims member purchase intent.
    (iii)Booster bundles have a tightened ATC rule (e.g., per-account purchase
         limit already hit elsewhere) -> server-side reject regardless of token.

  Note that the SAME warmup-tab session, 9 min later on the ETB drop, got a
  201 on its ATC. So whatever rejected the booster bundle is item-specific OR
  account-specific, not session-level.

FAILURE B: 95267143 ETB, ATC 201 then /checkout redirect-to-/cart + 429 on
  cart-delete with FAST_SELLING_ITEM_RATE_LIMIT_EXCEPTION.

  This is Target's hot-item reservation throttle (DCO = Digital Commerce
  Order, item-level rate-limit). The bot got the item INTO the cart, then
  the order-reservation tier refused the checkout because the per-item
  bucket was exhausted across all bots/users hammering the same SKU during
  the drop. There is nothing the bot can do about this except (a) be one of
  the very first to ATC, or (b) try multiple sessions in parallel from
  separate accounts. Our single-account single-browser purchase path will
  always lose this race against script kiddies and other bots on truly hot
  drops.

  The post-fail diagnosis "F5/Target rate-limit block (busy error on page)"
  fires when the cart page DOM contains 'busier' / 'busy right now' / etc.
  text. That diagnosis is plausibly correct here - busy modal + 429 on
  cart-delete are consistent with item-level scarcity throttle.

OTHER FINDINGS
================================================================================

F1 - CLOAKING ALARM is way too chatty
  1,082 alarm fires in 11h with ZERO true positives. Once a TCIN is "in stock
  earlier this run" the alarm trips on every 30s cycle where everything is
  OOS, which is the common case. The verify cache-bust costs ~150ms each
  (one extra fetch) - cumulatively that is ~5 minutes of useless work. The
  threshold should probably be ramped exponentially or reset after N cycles
  of confirmed-OOS. Not a correctness problem, just noise.

F2 - WARMUP CACHE OVER-EVICTION ON FIRST DROP
  Shape-ring evicted 14 stale captures at 02:15 (drop 2) - because the
  previous capture cycle had been running for two hours with no purchase
  activity, every capture in the ring aged past the 300s eviction window.
  The first hot drop of the run thus gets minimum-headroom headers. Either
  raise eviction window for low-purchase-frequency runs or refresh on a
  shorter cadence regardless of activity.

F3 - SESSION 6 (168.158.141.141) HAS A SLIGHT OUTLIER
  s6 recycled 13 times (others 7-8). No 403s tied to it specifically, but
  worth eyeballing if it persists.

F4 - WRAPPER ONLY RELAUNCHES AT MIDNIGHT
  bot_restart_wrapper.log shows ONE launch per day around 00:04-01:37 since
  5/24. There is no auto-relaunch when the manual shutdown happens at
  ~08:00-10:30 CT. The 13.5h daytime window is dark every day. If drops
  happen during daytime (rare for Target Pokemon TCG but not impossible),
  they are 100% missed.

F5 - PURCHASE_STATES.JSON STILL SHOWS 95298172 AS "ready"
  The booster bundle is still marked ready=true in purchase_states.json,
  meaning the bot will retry it on the next in-stock signal. If the 401
  root cause is account-level rather than transient, this will fail on
  every future drop until the cause is fixed. Worth investigating before
  the next overnight run.

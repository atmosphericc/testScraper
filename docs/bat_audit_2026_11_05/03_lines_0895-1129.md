## 1. Overview

This range configures four things.

- **Multi-SKU dispatch.** Decides how many of the bot's three logged-in accounts ("workers") are committed to each product (TCIN) when several products are in stock at once.
- **DCO burst re-POST.** A rapid repeat of the add-to-cart request after the first response.
- **Shape harvester and banked replay.** This is the biggest block. A background browser tab per account performs real, human-like clicks on a cheap in-stock product page so that the retailer's F5 Shape anti-bot script mints valid request-signing headers. The bot stores ("banks") those headers and replays one on the real add-to-cart shot. In security terms this is token/credential replay: a signed anti-automation token is harvested from a genuine interaction and reused on a programmatic request.
- **In-chain pre_checkout retry.** Keeps retrying the checkout-gate call quickly instead of falling back to a slow path.

Commentary is dated 2026-09-03 to 2026-10-14, with one note from 2026-11-04. Per the task, adversary behaviour cited here is dated evidence and may be stale.

## 3. Narrative by section

### 3.1 D1 multi-SKU dispatch (lines 895-959)

Background (2026-09-20): analysis of 104 run logs (tools/analysis/limiter_key.py) concluded Target's edge limiter behaves like a shared per-TCIN volume bucket, not a per-identity cooldown. Pass rate fell from 9.6 percent with no prior shots on that TCIN in 120 s, to 1.1 percent at 3-4, to 0.4 percent at 9 or more, and other identities suppressed ours even from different IPs. In security terms, the limiter counts requests against the product, not the client, so rotating identity or IP does not reset it. Yet the bot sent all 3 accounts at one TCIN 36 of 36 times on 09-17, while MULTI_SKU_MISS skipped 13 live hot TCINs on 09-16. Dispatch reserves workers synchronously before threads spawn so a lagging counter cannot hand one worker to two products.

The cap was then tuned with an audit trail.
- 09-21, 1 to 2: a fresh adversarial re-check of the evidence for the 1-worker cap (0.038 vs 0.545 admits) came back NOT ESTABLISHED. It reproduced only under an undisclosed scope, the decisive cells were 11 and 28 windows with 6 and 1 passes, the retry loop breaks on success so low-shot windows are partly windows that resolved early (reversed causality), and no window ever fired 1 account.
- 09-23, 2 to 3 (docs/CLAIMS.md C-0923-04): alt-1 fired zero shots all night because the cap gave primary and business all 9 races. Cost acknowledged (C-0924-01, verified 2026-09-24): a second TCIN going live while all three are racing the first gets MULTI_SKU_MISS and is not re-picked-up (23 such skips in 107 logs, 0 raced in the same window; 15 pairs in 27 nights, 2 in the hot era). Whether a third account adds an independent ticket is NOT ESTABLISHED because all three share the home IP.

Net effect: with WORKERS_PER_TCIN=3 equal to the 3-account fleet, the first live TCIN takes everyone, so CAP_ALWAYS=1 is effectively a no-op on a 3-account fleet. The reservation bookkeeping remains. Drop-day risk: a second hot TCIN flipping while the first is being raced is lost. This is a deliberate operator trade.

### 3.2 DCO burst re-POST and cart hold (lines 960-986)

A "burst" is rapid repeat add-to-cart POSTs after the first response (the DCO). Timeline:
- 09-18 first live night: 6 of 7 bursts went out page-signed (bank stale; the flip shot had consumed the only fresh banked set) and all 6 drew an edge 429. REQUIRE_BANK was added to fire only with a replayable banked set.
- 2026-10-11: set to 0 because a shot that passed the edge and drew FAST_SELLING should re-POST within 1.0-1.5 s even with an empty bank (10-09 04:32:35: the night's only admitted shot got no burst because the human-verification wall had emptied every bank). Evidence: 15/48 past the edge within 5 s of a DCO versus 0/7 at 45-90 s.
- 2026-11-04 caveat embedded in the REM: "does not reproduce on ident-tagged data, 3/33 vs 0/8; C-1104-04". The justification is weakened, but the flag was not changed.
- 2026-10-14 (audit A1 #9): BANK_WAIT_S 6 to 2 so bursts land within about 3.5 s. FX-1014-BURSTSTUCK: STUCK_NOWAIT=1 skips the wait when the harvester is stuck (PX park, back-off, buttonless streak); burst then fires at about 1.5 s.
- TARGET_CART_HOLD_CHECK_INTERVAL_S=1.0 (code default 4): no rationale in this range. It polls the cart hold four times as often (more requests to the retailer).

Risk: page-signed bursts were seen drawing 429 (6/6 on 09-18). Extra requests add to the shared per-TCIN volume the earlier analysis says suppresses success, and add a detection signal.

### 3.3 Real PDP referrer (lines 987-991)

Fetch's `Referer` header is a forbidden name and never reached the wire; shots carried the parked homepage/account URL. Using fetch's `referrer` init option puts the real product-page URL on the wire. Detection surface: referrer consistency is a basic bot-fingerprint check. Kill: 0.

### 3.4 Shape harvest and banked replay (lines 992-1081)

Premise (2026-09-03): every hot-SKU window since 08-07 died with 100 percent 401 _ERR_AUTH_DENIED on shots that passed the edge limiter. A byte audit found the main-tab ATC had carried the `-a0` behavioural chunk 0 of 53,950 times and never a real page click. The harvester (src/session/shape_harvest.py) drives human-trajectory CDP clicks on a cheap in-stock PDP, the interceptor captures the page-signed X-headers and fails the request before it leaves Chrome (nothing lands, uuid unspent), banks sets, and Fetch.continueRequest swaps one in on the real shot. Boot self-test replays on the warmup dummy POST: 424 means accepted, 3 of 3 returning 401/403 turns replay off for the run (shots stay page-signed). Empty bank mid-window means a page-signed shot.

Security view: this is replay of a signed anti-automation token minted by synthetic-but-realistic input. Risks: detection of repeated identical trajectories, token reuse, per-account behavioural flagging. The REM cites vendor guidance (Refract) that stacking sets far past about 3 per running task raises flag risk, so BANK=6 is described as "a measured shortage fix, not a free lunch".

Values and rationale:
- BANK 3 to 6 (09-20): bank held a replayable set only 21-26 percent of the time; a live window spends about 3 sets in 4 s while the harvester mints one per about 10 s. Rollback: 3.
- TTL 300; MAX_REPLAY_AGE 100 to 300 (09-20): the 100 s cap was an unmeasured assumption. Primary's edge passes showed no decay with set age inside 100 s (0-10 s 4/20, 11-30 s 2/20, 31-60 s 3/23, 61-100 s 2/5), and vendors publish 5-15 minute lives (Refract "10-15 minutes", Stellar 300000 ms). Evidence above 100 s is vendor claims only, not measured on this target. Readout: if pass rates fall at ages over 100 s, restore 100.
- HARVEST_SKIP empty (09-09): all accounts harvest. The 09-04 premise "primary fails under sweep load" was the hidden-tab bug. Root cause (09-07 note, supersedes 09-08): every Target.createTarget opens a foreground tab, so after each ~75-minute sentinel Chrome restart the harvest tab was pushed behind; a hidden tab paints no frames and Chromium releases queued mouse input only via a 5 s rAF fallback timer (4,613 click timeouts). Fix: visibility probe and re-activation before each click (never over a PARKED account where a person may be solving a challenge), abort when one move stalls over 2500 ms, close dropped tabs. Operational rule: never minimize account Chrome windows while the harvester runs.
- TAB_OPEN 45 s and CLICK 12 s only matter beyond zendriver's own 10 s wait.
- PREFER_SHIPPING (09-09 audit): every 09-07 capture was a STORE_PICKUP add.
- BYTEMATCH (09-09): sends the page's own ATC URL (%2C + key=) so the request matches a real one byte for byte (fingerprint parity).
- HARVEST_TCINS: three staples, "swap for any cheap in-stock ship-eligible item". Drop-day risk: if all three go out of stock or non-ship-eligible, the loop rotates and logs; if none works the bank empties and shots go page-signed.

### 3.5 In-chain pre_checkout retry (lines 1082-1106)

Evidence (09-20, 104 logs): carts whose in-chain pre_checkout was FAST_SELLING-rejected converted 0 of 8; carts with in-chain 2xx converted 14 of 34. All four September hot carts read atc=201 pre=429 po=0 skip=pre_429. On 09-18 the same pre_checkout returned 201 at +7.5 s and +12.4 s, but by then place-order drew RESERVATION_FAILURE (units gone). Every order ever placed finished in 2.3-4.0 s. The JS chain now retries pre_checkout itself (up to 5 tries, 250 ms gap, 1500 ms budget) and fires place-order in the same chain on the first 2xx. Only 429 is retried; 401/424/400 hand back to the legacy path. pre_checkout carries no Shape headers and the throttle answers in 5-14 ms, so retries are cheap. Readout: "pre_tries=" on the chain-done line. Kill: PRE_RETRY=0 (JS byte-identical to golden).

### 3.6 Fresh page per harvest click (lines 1107-1129)

2026-09-13 forensics (run_20260911): every win in history rode a SMALL first-click Shape set (no -a0 chunk). A harvest tab clicking the same PDP for about 15 minutes overflowed the 7,900-char `-a` into `-a0` with about 13 minutes of synthetic telemetry; those sets went 0 of 14 on limiter-passing shots, and the night's only 201 rode a set captured 2 s after a PDP reload. The -a0 bytes also pushed 380 cart_items POSTs over the edge's 431 header cap. FRESH_PAGE reloads the PDP before every harvest click; _LIVE=1 allows that during a live window; MIN_GAP bounds PDP load rate; PREFER_NO_A0 makes the shot pick the freshest no-a0 set over a fresher bloated one. If [WEDGE-PROBE]/TAB_HEALTH lines cluster around reloads, set _LIVE=0. Risk: reloads add traffic to the retailer (rate-limit and detection signal) and navigate during a live purchase.

## 6. Readiness notes

1. Multi-SKU cap is neutralised. WORKERS_PER_TCIN=3 on a 3-account fleet means a second simultaneous hot TCIN is skipped and not re-picked-up. If the next restock has several hot TCINs flipping close together, only the first is raced. If fewer than 3 accounts are logged in, the interplay with CAP_ALWAYS was not examined here.
2. Burst REQUIRE_BANK=0 rests on 10-09/10-11 evidence that the operator's own 11-04 re-analysis says does not reproduce. Decide whether to keep it; page-signed bursts drew 429 6 of 6 on 09-18.
3. Replay age 300 s rests on vendor documentation and a measurement valid only up to 100 s. A stale-token replay yields 401 that looks like any other Shape reject; the self-test only guards at boot, not mid-run.
4. HARVEST_TCINS (21516452, 50225561, 53274278) were chosen 09-03. Verify they are still in stock and ship-eligible before the restock.
5. The .bat is the single source of truth: code defaults for the important flags are off or weaker (table above). A lost or typo'd `set` line silently reverts to the weaker path.
6. FRESH_PAGE_LIVE=1 permits PDP navigation during a live purchase window; the REM's watch-items (WEDGE-PROBE/TAB_HEALTH around reloads) have no recorded readout in this range.

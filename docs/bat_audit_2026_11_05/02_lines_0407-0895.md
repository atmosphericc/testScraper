## 1. Overview

This range is the checkout and post-add-to-cart (ATC) half of the launcher's configuration. The operator keeps the file as a changelog, so each flag has a REM block naming the incident that produced it. It covers six areas:

- The fast-lane checkout chain (ATC -> awaited pre_checkout -> place-order in one in-browser fetch sequence).
- FAST_SELLING handling (Target's per-item throttle): cooldown, holding a won cart, the "won-cart ride".
- Browser (CDP, Chrome DevTools Protocol) health, wedge mitigation and leak guards.
- CVV challenge handling (Target challenging the saved card).
- Post-drop audit fixes, including several features deliberately switched OFF after being proven useless or harmful.
- Retry doctrine: "wave-first" versus spamming, 401 pulse, OOS stop, stuck-reset guard, and the DCO burst.

Security framing: most of it is about not tripping Target's detection surfaces. These are a per-TCIN edge rate limiter (429), F5 Shape token scoring per identity (surfaces as 401), and a cart/demand throttle (FAST_SELLING / DCO 429). The recurring theme is that request volume per identity is a cost, not a lever.

Cosmetic batch artifacts: `%%` in REM text is an escaped percent sign. `tests<TAB>est_...` (lines 423 and 507 area) is a mangled `tests\test_...` path.

## 3. Narrative by section

### 3.1 Checkout fast lane (407-448)

On the 2026-07-20 drop the ATC returned 201 at t=0.92s, but the first place-order POST did not leave until t=3.4s and Target answered it 429 RESERVATION_FAILURE. The 2.5s gap was a `/checkout/start` navigation (1.45s) plus a DOM poll for the Place Order button (0.87s); the inventory reservation expired inside it. Measured ATC->shot#1 ranged 2.4s to 10.1s across waves.

The fix chains ATC -> awaited pre_checkout -> place-order as one in-browser `fetch` sequence with no navigation and no CDP round trip, about 0.85s total. An earlier 2026-05-07 attempt to drop the navigation had failed with 424 CART_COMPARISION_FAILURE because it raced an un-awaited pre_checkout.

Safety properties (the double-purchase blast-radius guards): a 2xx is a committed order and is never re-shot; a fired-but-no-response POST bails terminal and is never retried; the chain will not fire unless ATC was 201, pre_checkout was 2xx and every cart item is our TCIN.

In-lane CVV (07-28): Target challenges the saved card with `400 MISSING_CREDIT_CARD_CVV`. The DOM modal's network call was captured live on 07-24 and 07-28 as `PUT /checkout_payments/v1/payment_instructions/{id}` carrying the CVV and no Shape headers. The lane now fires that PUT itself, taking the CVV from the per-account `cvv` field in `config\target_accounts.json`. This is credential replay with the card CVV held in plaintext config.

Risk if wrong: every won cart can be lost to a guaranteed 400, or the lane misbehaves. `=0` restores the exact prior behaviour.

### 3.2 FAST_SELLING back-off and egress (450-492)

FAST_SELLING_ITEM_RATE_LIMIT_EXCEPTION is Target's per-item throttle. On 07-21 it answered 0 of about 20 re-shoots and never appeared on a wave's first POST; the re-shoot loop was feeding the limiter and 2 minutes of quiet cleared it. So the executor now backs off 45s. On 2026-10-14 (C-1014-15) the back-off was found to be per account, so a throttle on item A pushed the account's shot on item B off the fast lane; PER_TCIN=1 scopes it to the item.

The egress bind (492) is NOT ARMED. It would route one account's purchase Chrome out a second adapter (phone hotspot on "Wi-Fi") for a different source IP (EXP-1002-NET). A pre-arming verifier on 2026-10-14 refuted it on four grounds:
1. Chrome's silent resend over the home line could re-send a committed place-order (double-order risk).
2. The Wi-Fi interface metric equals Ethernet's (25), so joining a hotspot could move the default route for every account and the monitor.
3. The account becomes "proxied" and is relaunched every 35 minutes.
4. A dead hotspot costs a 10s dial per connection.

Mitigations shipped at 22:5x: home fallback OFF by default; the bot refuses the bind unless the Wi-Fi adapter ranks behind home (one-time admin run of `tools\ops\hotspot_egress_setup.ps1`); bound dials give up after 4s. Logins still exit via the home IP. The file leaves it empty, so there is no egress change.

### 3.3 Hold the won cart (494-521)

On 07-28 the bot won 4 carts and cleared all of them after FAST_SELLING, then went 0-for-~250 re-racing ATC. A won cart is worth more than a re-race, so the executor now waits out the cooldown in place and re-shoots into the reopened window. The 09-11 drop (0-for) added multi-cycle holds and the WON_CART_RIDE flag, which lets the manager outlast its 150s future and 200s force-complete timers while a live cart is held. On 10-14 the ride cap was kept at 300s on purpose: a won-cart call holds its TCIN away from the other accounts until it returns, so 900s would lock them out about 13 minutes. Cycles and total are 6 and 270s.

Risk: a long hold monopolizes the TCIN for other accounts for up to roughly 4.5 min per call, and wastes that time if the cart is actually dead (the empty-cart bail in 3.6 covers that).

### 3.4 CDP and Chrome health (523-593)

- **Backpressure guard (07-20).** The session sentinel destroyed a healthy Chrome 23 times overnight. Warmup tabs' persistent Fetch interceptors flooded the single CDP websocket, so an idle probe queued past its 2s budget and was read as "wedged". Each false positive cost about 28s of blocked purchases plus the warm Shape header cache. `TAB_HEALTH_SLOW_RETRY_S=6.0` re-probes once with a longer budget.
- **Wedge mitigations (09-10).** An 18h soak showed CDP dying 49-84 min after launch (median 69), only on the two PROXIED account Chromes; the home-IP primary ran the whole 18h clean. The occlusion theory was falsified. `CHROME_MAX_AGE_S=2100` relaunches proxied Chromes at 35 min (a scheduled ~5s blip instead of a random 70-135s outage on the drop), inside the sentinel drop-guard so it cannot fire under a shot. `WEDGE_FAST_RESTART=1` restarts on strike 1 once the wedge-probe confirms a stall, instead of waiting for 3 strikes.
- **Refill on warm miss (09-10).** alt-1 ran 18h with no Shape-header refill because its boot warmup missed. The self-healing refill loop now starts anyway.
- **Paused-request leak (07-23).** A 07-21/22 run showed wedges on a rigid clock (70-75 min after each Chrome's own launch) with 22 sentinel destroys. The candidate root cause was dedup-hit events dropped without `Fetch.continueRequest`, leaving requests paused forever. The handler now continues them and logs "dedup hit #N". The REM says to confirm from the log: counter climbing with wedges gone confirms it, counter near 0 with wedges persisting means look elsewhere.

Risk: a wedge near the drop costs 70-135s of blocked purchasing on that account; proxied accounts are the exposed ones.

### 3.5 CVV challenge handling (595-653)

Target began challenging the saved card (07-20/21: 0-for-9 despite nine clean ATC 201s).
- **DOM_FIRST.** When latched, go DOM-first, where the CVV modal handler answers in about 0.08s. The latch is persisted to `state\cvv_challenge_<account>.flag`.
- **AUTO_UNLATCH.** While latched the API path is skipped, so the latch could never see Target drop the challenge. A DOM order that confirms with no CVV modal clears it. A wrong un-latch costs one ~0.9s 400 and re-latches.
- **RESERVATION_BAIL.** A RESERVATION_FAILURE rendered as Target's generic "busy" copy, so the DOM loop re-clicked 15 times into a dead reservation. It now bails.
- **WARMUP_PAUSE_DURING_PURCHASE.** Labelled a HYPOTHESIS. Target's help text says CVV re-entry is required when a shipping address is updated during checkout; a warmup `/cart` nav fires a cart PUT with `field_groups=ADDRESSES`, which landed just before every 400. Warmup tabs now skip the navigation (not the dummy POST) while a purchase is live.
- **API_CAPTURE_CHECKOUT_STEPS=false.** Passive capture of Target's CVV PUT was turned off 08-02: the body was captured and Endpoint 8 live-validated on 07-31, and the capture had logged 10,840 CART_PUTs (~11.5MB) from warmup activity. Re-enable only if Target changes the endpoint.

Risk: a changed Target CVV flow could silently make the lane a guaranteed 400.

### 3.6 2026-08-02 post-drop-audit guards (655-697)

The REM says these are default-ON in code and set explicitly so the file stays the changelog (07-31 drop: 7 orders / 14 units).
- **EMPTY_CART_BAIL.** After a 424 RESERVATION_FAILURE, Target moves cart items to "Saved for later". Late 07-31 waves burned 15-25s each re-clicking that empty cart. It now bails on the wire signature.
- **RESHOOT_RENAV.** The SPA bounced the tab to /cart mid-hold and the re-shoot loop had zero shots. It now does one re-nav to /checkout.
- **LEVEL_REARM_S=3** (20 on 08-02, 3 since 08-31). Resilient mode published stock events only on OOS->IS flips, so a failed wave never re-raced while stock persisted. The 08-28 audit measured 11.6s median lag from poll quantization; at 3s the boundary drops to about 1.5-6.5s, and the poll reads in-memory state so cost is nil.
- **SENTINEL_DEAD_SESSION_BACKOFF_S=1800.** A dead login session had the sentinel restart that Chrome every 5 min forever (576 restarts on 08-02), hammering Target's login surface (a lockout/detection risk). Heavy rungs are now parked 30 min.
- **CLOAK_VERIFY_BACKOFF.** The cloaking alarm fired every 30s for 62.5h (~67k extra origin fetches). It widens to 300s after a confirmed all-OOS.

### 3.7 Things deliberately switched OFF (699-772)

- **ATC_NATIVE_FALLBACK=0** (08-07). The theory was that page-signed fetches are exempt from Shape's per-identity lock. The 08-06/07 restock went 0-for-3-accounts (0 carts on about 2,960 ATC) and the fallback fired 1,468 times returning 401/429 every time. The real gate is per-identity anti-bot and demand on the hot SKU.
- **ATC_401_LADDER=0** (08-21). The ladder cost 2.6-9.3s per 401; 0/85 mints and 0/103 in-ladder retries converted.
- **ATC_DEAD_TOKEN_MIDWINDOW_REPAIR=1.** Safety net for a truly dead write token. The 2026-10-18 correction (C-1018-16) says "confirmed dead" (three keyless 401s in a row) is also produced by the Shape verdict; all 30 DEADs on 10-09 and 10-16 were false. It is inert only because `TARGET_TOKEN_KEEPFRESH=0` (line 1691, outside this range) makes the repair a no-op. Never re-arm keep-fresh, or each false DEAD forces a token refresh (the 09-28 class).
- **RETRY_FORCE_REWARM=0** (08-21). The forced reload fired a /cart load plus an ADDRESSES cart PUT (the CVV trigger) between every retry (26 navs / 27 PUTs for 30 shots).
- **Items 10-13 from the 08-28 Friday stack** (14 windows / 2,680 shots / 0 successes): RETRY_WARM=0, CART_HOLD_SKIP_EDGE=1, SHOT_TTL_REFRESH=0, ATC_DCO_AS_EDGE_CADENCE=1. Findings: 84% of shots were empty-429 from the per-TCIN edge limiter; 100% of shots that passed the limiter died 401 (TCIN-scoped Shape score); wave-first shots converted 32.6% versus 0.9% for later re-POSTs. The per-retry dummy POST taxed 0.3-2.0s and produced 17/17 false "dead token" repairs. Removing it gave about +40% tickets per identity and about 25% less cookie burn ("more cookies = more flags" is attributed to Refract, reported not verified).

### 3.8 Wave-first doctrine and 401 pulse (774-862)

- **401_PULSE** (item 14): after 3 consecutive carts-401s pause 15-25s so the next shot re-enters wave-first. Edge 429s neither count nor reset the streak. It is INERT under WAVE_FIRST_ONLY=1 and was superseded by `TARGET_IDENTITY_REST`, which is NOT armed (0 at line 1537).
- **WAVE_FIRST_ONLY=1** (09-09 census: 21,684 ATC POSTs, 1,609 windows, 20 orders). 19/20 orders landed on attempt #1 at t+0 and 0/13,244 at attempt >= 2. A fresh shot after >= 5 prior shots on the TCIN within 120s went 0-for-438. On hyped SKUs the 429 share rose from 30% to 90% by shot 3-5, i.e. the bot's own volume trips the per-TCIN limiter.
- **WAVE_FIRST_EDGE** has confusing prose. On 09-23 it was set 1 -> 0 as a BET (re-fire edge/DCO 429s at 2-3s cadence, about 40 tickets per account instead of 2; the REM says NOT ESTABLISHED either way). On 09-25 the pre-registered readout killed it: 663 re-shots at window age 2-120s produced zero edge admissions (C-0925-01) and all 5 edge admissions that night were a window's first shot. The current value is back at **1**.
- **SHOT_BANK_GATE=1 / WAIT_S=8.** A cold shot waits up to 8s for a fresh real-click Shape set. A page-signed cold shot still fires.
- **RETRY_STOP_WHEN_OOS=1 / OOS_STOP_S=8** (09-23 A3, C-0923-03 VERIFIED). 8 of 37 shots went out 0.1-19s after the monitor had read the TCIN out of stock. A race now ends when the monitor has had no in-stock read for 8s; it fails open on missing or stale data.
- **STUCK_RESET_LIVE_GUARD=1** (09-23 G1). Two 60s stuck-purchase resets read only the state file, so a live racer could be reset to ready and a second race opened on the same TCIN, re-claiming the same accounts. It had already fired on live races 6 times, harmless only because multi-SKU dispatch was off.

### 3.9 DCO burst (863-894)

A DCO-body 429 ("Request throttled due to high demand item") on the cart_items POST means the edge limiter ADMITTED the shot and the cart service's demand throttle rejected it. Pre-09-17 data showed a re-POST within 5s got past the edge again 15/48 times, versus 0/7 after 45-90s. The 2026-11-04 note in the REM says those counts are pre-09-17 data, and that in the flip era 09-30..10-29 the same re-POST got past the edge only 1/19 (docs/CLAIMS.md C-1104-04). The burst is kept short on purpose (MAX=2, gap 1.0-1.5s, only with a banked Shape set). The census puts our 2nd-3rd shot in its best bucket (20% edge-pass) and the 6th+ in its worst (2%).

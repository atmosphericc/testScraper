# run_bot_with_nightly_restart.bat, lines 1129-1587

Audit date: 2026-11-05. Scope: lines 1129 through 1587 only. Code verification greps were run over `src/`, `app.py` and `tools/` (all consumers turned out to live in `src/` and `app.py`).

## 1. Overview

This range is the middle of the launcher's "arming" section. It is almost all environment-variable flags that switch on defensive and tactical behaviour added from September to November 2026. Four themes run through it.

- **Order-safety guards.** Latches that stop the bot placing a second order when the first order's outcome is unknown. In security terms these are idempotency and double-spend protections.
- **The "won-cart" loop.** Once an add-to-cart (ATC) succeeds on a contested product, the bot keeps hammering checkout and place-order requests at a fixed cadence until the product goes out of stock.
- **Multi-SKU fairness and re-arm.** How the three accounts are shared across several simultaneously live products.
- **Session-health and anti-bot hygiene.** Login probes, guest-jar guards (credential and session integrity), the "sentinel" that periodically checks each login, and the Shape and PerimeterX/HUMAN "harvest" machinery.

The tail of the range is a long REM-only block. It records the retired "park alt-1/business off hot SKUs" experiment, the not-armed alternatives, and the per-TCIN quantity pin. Every Target anti-bot claim below is a dated operator observation from the REM text, not a current fact. The dates are given so you can judge staleness.

Glossary for a security reader:
- **TCIN:** Target's product ID.
- **Account idents:** `primary`, `alt-1` and `business` are the three logged-in accounts.
- **Edge 429 / limiter:** the per-TCIN rate limiter at Target's CDN edge. The bot treats each request as a "draw" in a lottery.
- **Shape / HUMAN (PerimeterX, "PX"):** the bot-detection vendors. Shape signs requests with telemetry. PX serves challenge pages.
- **Bank / harvest:** pre-minted, Shape-signed request material ("credential replay"), collected by idle Chrome tabs and spent at drop time.
- **Quiet mode:** a state where background browser activity is suppressed so it does not collide with the live purchase.

## 3. Narrative by section

### 3.1 2026-09-17 HOT-SKU FIX ARMING (lines 1129-1158): never double-buy
This block follows the 09-16 "0-for" (a drop with zero orders). The header says every flag defaults to the prior behaviour in code, so each kill is the value named in its note.

- **AC-1 ambiguous-commit latch** (1142-1147). If a place-order request is sent and no answer comes back, the order may or may not exist. The bot latches that account and TCIN off for 30 minutes and persists the latch to disk, so a crash and wrapper relaunch cannot race a second order. This is the retail equivalent of an idempotency key. The operator's check is `grep [AMBIGUOUS_COMMIT]` (expect 0) and then order history.
- **PO_5XX_AMBIGUOUS** (1153). A gateway 504 can arrive after the order was committed, so 408/5xx is treated like no answer.
- **RACE_STATE_STARTED_AT_GUARD** (1156). A bug-class guard. A missing timestamp is stamped now rather than computed as an age since 1970, which would force-complete a race immediately.
- **If wrong on drop day:** a kill (=0) removes the double-order protection, and the won-cart loop refuses to arm. Leaving them on has the opposite cost: a false ambiguity locks an account out of a TCIN for 30 minutes during a live window.

### 3.2 WC-1 won-cart direct checkout loop (lines 1159-1275)
**What it is.** Once a cart is "won" (ATC returned 2xx), the bot used to go through a ~26 s browser navigation and DOM-click detour with 45 s holds. WC-1 instead fires "tickets" (pre_checkout / place-order-only requests) directly at a fixed cadence while RedSky says the product is in stock.

**Why the cadence changed three times.**
- **R5 (2026-09-17).** The old 45 s gap came from a 07-21 observation that re-shooting into FAST_SELLING was 0-for-~20. The operator argued that sample was confounded by MISSING_CREDIT_CARD_CVV 400s (fixed 07-28). The only two drops with zero CVV failures (07-31, 9 orders; 08-04, 4 orders) were the only ones that converted, at a ~5-6 s median gap. R5 therefore moved to 3,4,5 then every 5 s.
- **2026-09-18 read-out.** Both carts that were won got one checkout draw in the first 3 s, then waited. About 22 percent of gate-facing requests passed. None of 8 carts whose chain pre_checkout was FS-rejected ever converted, and all 14 orders were one chain done in about 3 s. The schedule became draws at +1,+2,+3,+5,+7,+10 s then every 3 s with 1 s jitter.
- **2026-09-25.** Added RF_ENTRY. A 429 RESERVATION_FAILURE now also enters the loop, after a night where such a cart fell to the slow legacy path. The comment itself says "Evidence it converts a hot cart: NOT ESTABLISHED" (2 recoveries, both 08-04).

**RIDE-TO-OOS (2026-10-14, lines 1222-1239).** Ten of 11 loops that ended while the product was still live ended on the bot's own caps. The operator wants to mimic "leading bots" and keep submitting until out of stock. YIELD_FLEET goes to 0. The 40-ticket and 120 s caps are exempt while the stock probe reads live. The hard ceiling is 1200 tickets (~1 h at ~3 s). The note says: "Never a second order: more place-orders on the same cart".

**Eviction read and presume** (1240-1258). A place-order 424 RESERVATION_FAILURE empties the cart. The loop used to keep firing 30 or more tickets at an empty cart, and the cart GET itself was rate limited. Now the loop reads the cart, and if the read fails twice it presumes the cart was evicted. The comment cites 6 of 6 recorded 424s having emptied the cart.

**Related items.**
- HARVEST_QUIET_RECHECK (1265) fixes a leak where a harvest run clicked ATC mid-won-cart.
- HELD_CART_REENTRY and TTL=3600 (1270-1275) re-enter a still-held cart for up to an hour. TTL was raised from 900 because 10-01 windows ran ~3,760 s and ~3,938 s.
- HELD_LINE_FLIP_STRIKE (1285): if the line is already in the cart at a new flip (ATC says MAX_PURCHASE), go straight to pre_checkout and place-order if the cart holds only our TCIN at qty at most Q.

**Security framing.** The ride-to-OOS loop sends about 20 checkout-endpoint POSTs a minute per won cart for up to an hour. That maximises draws against Target's per-cart and per-TCIN limiters, but it is also a loud, regular, long-lived pattern from one session, which is what velocity and behavioural rules key on. The REM records no incident from it yet.

**If wrong on drop day.** Too aggressive risks account flags or a 429/ban on the home IP. Too conservative abandons a won cart, which was the dominant failure on 09-16.

### 3.3 Gate-hardening audit (lines 1276-1306, 2026-10-04/05)
- **BOOT_SKIP_FAILED_W1.** If Worker 1 fails the boot login probe (a genuine no-greeting answer), relaunch without it. A second failure clears the list, and a real login clears it. Otherwise it expires after 12 h.
- **PO_2XX_AMBIGUOUS.** A place-order 2xx other than 200/201 is treated as maybe placed and never fired again on that cart.
- **FOREIGN_KEEP_WON.** Delete only the foreign line from a cart that also contains another item, and re-read the cart before each ticket, rather than clearing the whole cart. The code makes this inert unless PO_2XX_AMBIGUOUS is also 1.
- **ATC_NET_META.** Log-only network telemetry per ATC: wire send time, connection id and reuse, edge IP, ttfb. The latest commit message (9959a895) also uses it to capture the Fastly POP for the "line-key question".

### 3.4 Many-SKU restock fixes (lines 1307-1348, 2026-10-06 and 10-14)
**Problem.** With 3 workers per TCIN, the first TCIN in the RedSky response took all 3 accounts. Every other live TCIN printed MULTI_SKU_MISS and got zero shots (21 of 51 multi-TCIN windows).

**Fixes, all armed.**
- FAIR_SHARE splits accounts when several TCINs flip together.
- MISS_REARM keeps a re-arm hint for a TCIN that got no account.
- STOCK_RESET_KEEP_LIVE_FAILED stops a live failed TCIN from being dropped.
- WAVE_FIRST_YIELD_MISSED lets a waiting racer end early to cover a skipped TCIN.
- SHARED_LOCK_ORDER_FIX removes a lock-ordering deadlock between the stock handler and a finishing racer. The code comment says the deadlock would stall the EventBus.
- REBUY_NONBUYERS (operator 10-14: "more units is fine") keeps a bought TCIN offered to the other accounts. The buyer, and any account with a possibly-committed order, sits out until a new window opens. Buyers persist in state\rebuy_buyers.json. The verifier's residual risk is stated as NOT ESTABLISHED: a committed order answered as a non-ambiguous non-2xx.

**Risk.** Rebuy can create multiple orders by design. Check order history after a purchase.

### 3.5 FX-1011-CADENCE fast re-shoot (lines 1349-1373): armed, reverted, re-armed
- **10-11.** The fleet was armed to re-shoot 3.0-4.5 s after an edge 429, up from 55-70 s. On 10-09, 686 of 690 POSTs drew the edge 429. The limiter state exists per TCIN before first contact, so every shot is a draw. The operator wrote "no need for a flag".
- **10-22 REVERTED to 0.** On its one armed night (10-16), 0/19 races passed wall 1 vs 4/136 before, and it drained the bank (869 of 1,151 re-shots page-signed). Experiment E2 (verifier PARTIALLY CONFIRMED) says Shape admits about the first 6 protected POSTs on a product from this machine, then answers keyless 401 for 20-50 min at any cadence. Fast re-shots burn that budget in seconds.
- **10-23 RE-ARMED for alt-1 only** as an A/B, with primary and business slow as the control. Readout R-FAST-1023 is "NO-DATA when the next window is closed".

**Wording trap.** The first paragraph still reads "on for every account". The final `set` is `alt-1`. Trust the set line.

### 3.6 Diagnostics and thread safety (lines 1374-1383)
STALL_WATCHDOG (stack dump on a held lock for 45 s or a stalled sweep for 90 s, plus [HEALTH] every 5 min) and THREAD_START_GUARD (purchase_thread before stock_thread, each in try/except, at most 3 retries) are reliability and observability flags. FX-1014-KICK (TARGET_REARM_KICK) is explicitly NOT armed. Two opus rounds refuted it because yields arrive in bursts and a kick hands the flip to one account alone.

### 3.7 Login and session integrity (lines 1384-1426)
- **LOGIN_CHECK_TIMEOUT_S=30** (10-08). Four boots in a row exited 87 because the homepage "Hi," greeting probe timed out at 10 s while the jars were MEMBER.
- **LOGIN_PROBE_ACCOUNT_FALLBACK=0** (disarmed 23:58 the same night). Readiness showed primary and business were GUEST (sut=G). The /account URL check cannot tell guest from member, so the fallback would boot guest sessions. This is a session-confusion hazard: a guest session cannot place member orders.
- **JAR_GUEST_GUARD.** Never overwrite a member cookie jar (sut=R) with a guest jar (sut=G). On 10-14 the wrapper relogin pass and the in-bot sentinel saved guest cookies over target*.json without reading sut.
- **SENTINEL_MEMBER_CHECK.** The sentinel's ladder passed on "Account"/"Orders" text, which a guest page also shows. Two guest accounts logged logged_in=True all morning on 10-14. It now reads the live accessToken.
- **SENTINEL_STAGGER_S=30.** Each check holds that account's page lock for ~2 s. All three were checked within 3 ms, so a flip landing then waited on every account.
- **SENTINEL_DEFER (2026-11-04, the newest flag in the range).** The sentinel's 300 s /account navigation holds the page lock. It collided with the window open in 3 of 5 windows on 10-30, giving first-shot delays of 2,182 / 7,996 / 621 / 1,927 ms vs 28-47 ms unaffected. It skips the navigation when any TCIN is live, a window opened under 180 s ago, or local time is 01:30-04:30, only when the member token has a TTL of at least 900 s. An unknown, guest or expired token always runs the check. Pre-registered PASS: every ident at most 150 ms in at least 90 percent of windows, with no ERR_UNAUTHORIZED or guest increase vs run_20261029_231605. Knobs: DEFER_RECENT_S=180, QUIET_LOCAL=01:30-04:30, DEFER_MIN_TOKEN_S=900.
  - **Residual risk:** the sentinel is the only periodic guest detector. Skipping it in the quiet window and during live stock means a session that silently degrades to guest may go unnoticed.
  - **Validation:** verified offline (80/80) and by claims-verifiers, but no live validation yet.

### 3.8 Monitor logging and WC-2 hygiene (lines 1427-1450)
- **Log-only flags:** RESILIENT_OTHER_LOG and RESILIENT_SUBNET_STEER_LOG.
- **WC-2 hygiene.** While a cart is held: no /cart re-warm, no warmup navs, no fleet cycle-warm, no duplicate pre_checkout. A ride ends as won_cart_ride_timeout instead of a false purchase_impl_hang browser restart. In security terms, extra background traffic on a session that holds a cart is wire noise and a way to disturb the held reservation.
- **FASTLANE_STAGE_TRACK (FL-1).** On a 12 s evaluate timeout, a 2 s read aborts the chain unless place-order already started, so a stuck ATC is retried and not treated as a possible double-buy.

### 3.9 DX-1 telemetry (lines 1451-1459)
Six log-only flags: [EXPOSURE], [IDENT_CENSUS], browser timestamps, [FS_TICKET], cart_qty, RedSky pickup fields. They feed post-run analysis and cost only log volume.

### 3.10 HV-1 harvest and PerimeterX handling (lines 1460-1489)
This is about the pre-minted signed requests ("bank") and the idle Chrome "harvesters" that produce them.

- **Skip disables replay.** A SKIPped account also turns replay off.
- **MISS_PROBE.** A harvest load with no button is probed once per nav, with at most 10 screenshots a run. If the page is a PerimeterX challenge, the harvester is parked for 300 s with no nav and no click. The park is clamped to 30-3600 s.
- **BANK_GATE_ADAPTIVE.** The bank gate stops waiting while the harvest is stuck.
- **FLUSH_ON_RELAUNCH.** A relaunch flushes the bank.
- **MISS_RENAV_LIVE=0.** Live re-nav on a miss stays off until probe data exists.
- **PX_RENAV (2026-10-14).** On 10-09 three accounts re-probed one challenged document 139 times over ~4 h and ended with no banked set. A challenge can clear in place (10-05), so the first park stays. A second verdict on the same document loads the next decoy. Parks double up to 3600 s, at most 3 such loads an hour, and never while a purchase is live or a won-cart loop is running.
- **SENTINEL_LOG_SKIPS.** Log-only.

## 5. Comment vs code drift

1. **Header claim at lines ~1133-1135** ("Every flag below defaults to the prior behaviour in code ... or deleting the line"). Mostly true, since most code defaults are '0'. Exceptions: `TARGET_PO_5XX_AMBIGUOUS` (code default '1', PE:322) and `TARGET_STOCK_PROBE` (code default '1', BPM:859). For those, setting 1 in the .bat is a no-op, and deleting the line does NOT disable them. `TARGET_RESHOOT_FORCE_REWARM` is the inverse: its code default is '1', so the .bat's 0 is what disarms it, and deleting that line would re-enable force-rewarm. `TARGET_WONCART_YIELD_FLEET` also defaults to '1' in code (PE:1168), so the .bat's 0 is load-bearing there too.

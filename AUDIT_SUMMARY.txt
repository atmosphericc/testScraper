THE SINGLE LARGEST DETECTION SIGNAL
================================================================================

Stock monitor fires requests at PERFECTLY REGULAR 1.0-second intervals:

  Request Timestamps (YOUR BOT):
  0ms, 333ms, 666ms, 1000ms, 1333ms, 1666ms, 2000ms...
  (Machine-perfect determinism, same pattern every run)

  Request Timestamps (HUMAN BROWSING):
  500ms, 1200ms, 3100ms, 2400ms, 800ms, 4500ms...
  (High variance, no pattern, unpredictable)

Akamai's behavioral sensor logs request timestamps. A perfectly regular pattern
is one of their highest-confidence bot indicators. This alone likely accounts
for 40-60% of your detections.

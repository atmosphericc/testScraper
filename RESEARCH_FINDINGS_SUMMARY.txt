MOST CRITICAL REMAINING VULNERABILITY
  "10-dispatcher aggregate request pattern"

  Root cause: Stock monitor fires 10 concurrent fetch() calls from same
  browser context, producing 6-20 req/sec frequency envelope. Akamai's
  behavioral sensor logs request timestamps; this aggregate pattern is
  inconsistent with human behavior regardless of exponential distribution
  within each dispatcher.

  Fix: Reduce NUM_DISPATCHERS from 10 to 3. Exponential variance within
  3 dispatchers is sufficient for speed (~7-15 checks/sec total) and masks
  the per-dispatcher pattern.

  Impact: Likely responsible for 20-30% of remaining blocks.
